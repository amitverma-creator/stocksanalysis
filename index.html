What every number means
Score (0–100)
Adds up trend (20-day average, 5 and 20-day moves), today's move, where the price sits in today's range, RSI, volume, nearness to the 52-week high, and how likely the stock is to move enough to reach your target. Higher is a better fit for a 4–5 day trade. It is a ranking, not a prediction.
RSI (14)
Momentum gauge from 0 to 100. 55–68 is healthy strength. Above 75 is overbought, so a pullback is more likely; below 45 is weak.
vs 20-day avg
How far the price is above its 20-session average. Positive means an uptrend; very high means stretched.
5-day / 20-day move
Recent price change over about one and four weeks. Steady gains beat sudden spikes.
Volume vs avg
Today's traded volume divided by the 20-day average. Above 1x means real participation. Early in the session it reads low because the day isn't finished.
Daily swing
Typical daily move (standard deviation of the last 20 days). Bigger swings can reach targets faster but also hit stop-losses faster.
Touch target / Touch stop
Chance the price touches your target or your stop-loss at some point within 5 sessions, from a standard random-walk formula using the stock's own swing. Both can happen, so they don't add up to 100%. These are statistical estimates and ignore news, results and market crashes.
Safety checks
A stock is skipped if it is below its 20-day average, overbought, weak, already up 7%+ today, falling today, or too calm to reach your target. If nothing passes, the system shows nothing rather than forcing a trade.
